How do the trial and lifetime unlock work?

Start the free 7-day trial from Settings → My Alarm Lifetime, or when saving your first plan. It starts when you confirm with Apple. After 7 days, adding, editing or enabling plans requires the one-time lifetime unlock (USD $1.99 base price; your App Store shows your local price). There is no subscription or automatic charge. Existing schedules keep working, and Stop, Snooze, Disable, Skip and Delete remain available. New calendar and weather reminders also require active access. Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the same Apple Account to recover the original trial or lifetime unlock; reinstalling does not restart the trial.

How do I change appearance?

Open Settings → Appearance and choose System, Light or Dark. System follows your iPhone’s appearance schedule. Time format and temperature units follow iPhone Language & Region settings.

How do I check ringing?

Open Settings → Alarms & permissions. Review alarm access and set a test alarm for one minute ahead. Lock your iPhone and listen. iOS draws the full-screen alarm and system Stop button. Notification-based reminders follow separate Silent and Focus settings.

How do future dates and skip work?

Use + for a new alarm and choose the date or repeating days. In Today, select a day to plan ahead. Skip changes one occurrence while keeping the routine. Reopen the app after skipped occurrences to refresh scheduling.

Why did the weather not change my alarm?

Weather needs optional approximate location, a forecast connection, and a refresh before the alarm. iOS controls background refresh timing. Open the app to refresh the plan and check Weather rules. Weather adjustments are not guaranteed.

Can an optional alarm check prevent Stop?

No. Math, shake, scan and photo checks are optional. iOS keeps its own Stop button, so a check cannot force you to complete it.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Plans are stored locally. Delete individual records in the app. To remove all local data, choose iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → My Alarm → Delete App; Offload App keeps documents and data. Manage any backups separately in Apple’s settings. There is no My Alarm account or server restore.

Do calendar and reminder imports change Apple’s apps?

No. Access is optional and read-only. Imported copies and generated alarms are local to My Alarm. Deleting the local copy does not remove your original Apple records.
