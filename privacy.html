My Alarm

Privacy policy

Updated October 21, 2026

Your plans stay with you

My Alarm is provided by True Solutions LLC. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated server for your plans. We do not collect or sell your alarm, reminder, calendar, camera, or motion data. The app does not send those records to us.

What is stored on your iPhone

Alarms, reminders, timers, templates, settings, skipped dates, and local reliability records are stored in the app’s local storage. The app shares the next alarm’s time and label and alarm-action state with its own widget and intents using an Apple app group. The app does not offer an account-based cloud sync service. Local records may be included in device backups managed by Apple, depending on your backup settings. Photo-check reference images are excluded from automatic device backup.

Optional weather and location

With permission, the app obtains your location while in use, rounds coordinates to about one kilometer, and caches that approximate location locally. The approximate coordinates are sent to Apple Weather through WeatherKit to request forecasts. We do not receive that location. Apple processes requests under its own privacy policy. Revoking location permission stops use of the cached coordinates for new forecasts and clears the location cache when the app observes the change. Forecast requests and downloading Apple’s attribution artwork require a network connection.

Calendar and reminders

Optional calendar access is used to read events, suggest alarms, and create local alarms you configure. Optional access to Apple Reminders lets you copy selected reminders into My Alarm. The app does not edit or delete your original calendar events or Apple Reminders. Copies you create remain in My Alarm until you delete them, even if you later revoke access to the original source.

Camera, codes, motion and speech

Camera access is used only when you choose a scan or photo alarm check. Code contents and reference photos are stored locally; code recognition, photo comparison, and motion checks run on the device. Camera images and motion readings are not sent to us or to a third-party AI service. On supported devices, optional generated timer lines use Apple’s on-device model; spoken reminders use system speech synthesis. These features do not use a developer-operated AI or transcription server.

Permissions and your choices

Alarm permission lets iOS ring alarms and timers. Notification permission enables notification reminders, backup alerts, and calendar suggestions. Optional calendar, reminders, camera, motion, and location permissions are requested for the feature that needs them. You can review or revoke access in iPhone Settings. Notifications, widgets, and system alarm screens can show your chosen labels; review your lock-screen settings if those labels are private.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual alarms, reminders, and timers in the app. To remove all local app data, use iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → My Alarm → Delete App. Offload App retains documents and data. Delete any backup copies separately using Apple’s backup controls. Removing local copies does not delete the original Apple calendar or reminder records. There is no My Alarm account to delete, and we cannot restore local plans from a server.

Support, website and changes

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and retain correspondence only as needed for support and related legal obligations; you can request deletion by contacting us. Avoid sending private alarm labels, calendar details, photos, or access codes. The public help website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard request information such as IP addresses under GitHub’s privacy statement. We add no website analytics or advertising cookies. This policy may change when the app’s practices change; the updated date will be shown here.
